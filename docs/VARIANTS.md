# Individual variant descriptions

These blocks can be used as model-file descriptions, or as separate listings if you prefer. For one family listing, use MODEL_DESCRIPTION.md and the variant table.

## Low profile - LED and cable guides (v8)

A lower-profile Pico 2 W mount for the MMS2 flap, intended for an internal soldered-USB installation. The board sits 4.2 mm above the flat inside face. It uses the centred position moved 3.3 mm towards the rounded end, with a short external BOOTSEL button, a 1.75 mm filament light-pipe hole and internal cable-tie guides. Left and right flaps are supplied. Use the short button from this folder.

Compared with v7, the PCB supports and button's internal tip are 1 mm shorter. The board position, 2.10 mm fitting pegs, 5 mm button opening, light-pipe hole and cable guides are retained. The light-pipe filament must also be 1 mm shorter: around 4.7 mm is a starting point for a flush outside end, trimmed to leave a gap before the LED.

## Raised - LED and cable guides (v7)

A raised Pico 2 W mount for the MMS2 flap, with the board 5.2 mm above the flat inside face. Includes an external BOOTSEL button, a 1.75 mm filament light-pipe hole and two internal cable-tie guides for the USB lead. Left and right flaps are supplied. Use the long button from this folder.

Compared with v6, this adds the LED light-pipe hole and USB cable guides while keeping the same centred/rearward board position, 2.10 mm fitting pegs and raised support height. Around 5.7 mm of filament is a starting point for a flush outside end, trimmed clear of the LED. Check the dimensions of your own USB lead if using the Pico's socket.

## Raised basic (v6, optional)

The simpler raised Pico 2 W mount for users who do not want a light-pipe opening or cable-tie guides. The board sits 5.2 mm above the flat inside face, with the same centred/rearward placement and external BOOTSEL access as the later variants. Left and right flaps are supplied. Use the long button from this folder.

Compared with v5, the PCB moves 3.3 mm towards the rounded end and sideways into the centre between the existing flap posts. Its supports are 1 mm taller and the button's internal tip is 1 mm longer. The button hole moves to match. This is the first layout selected for the public downloads.
