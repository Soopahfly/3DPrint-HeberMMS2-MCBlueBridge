# Low profile - LED and cable guides

Development revision: v8.

Choose the left or right flap for your installation and use the matching button_short.stl in this folder. The PCB standoff is 4.2 mm above the flat inside face; peg tips reach 5.7 mm. Peg fitting diameter is 2.10 mm. The 5.00 mm BOOTSEL aperture is common to all three published options.

Includes a 1.75 mm LED light-pipe hole and internal cable-tie bridges. For an outside-flush light pipe, start with about 4.7 mm of filament and trim to leave a small gap before the LED.

Insert the button from inside, then seat the Pico component-side towards the flap. The flange retains the button outwards; the fitted PCB captures it inwards. No separate retaining ring is needed. Check that BOOTSEL presses and releases freely.
