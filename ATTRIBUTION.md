# Attribution

The original MiSTer Multisystem2 enclosure was designed by **Richard Horne (RichRap), for Heber Ltd and The Retro Collective**, and published by Multisystem.

- [Original enclosure model](https://www.printables.com/model/1378481-mister-multisystem2-analogue-fpga-based-retrogamin)
- [Multisystem creator profile](https://www.printables.com/@Multisystem/models)
- Source licence: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA)**, as listed on the original model. This remix retains those terms.

The base flap files are `LEFT_FLAP_Multisystem2_V17_MASTER_C183_June23_2025_FINAL_PRODUCTION_FILES_RTP.stl` and `RIGHT_FLAP_Multisystem2_V17_MASTER_C183_June23_2025_FINAL_PRODUCTION_FILES_RTP.stl`. Their names match the original model's public file list; the attribution record is preserved in [docs/source_attribution_record.json](docs/source_attribution_record.json).

This remix adds Pico 2 W mounting posts and external BOOTSEL access, relocates and centres the board, provides two board heights with matching buttons, and offers LED light-pipe and USB cable-guide options. [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) documents the development revisions.

MC BlueBridge is a separate firmware project by **Anime0t4ku**: [MC-BlueBridge](https://github.com/Anime0t4ku/MC-BlueBridge). Firmware is not included in this mechanical-model repository.
